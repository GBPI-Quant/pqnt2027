This is us

PIQUANT GmbH was founded in Cologne in 2013. Its headquarters are located at the Rheinauhafen.
From the very beginning PIQUANT GmbH offers high quality IT-consulting.
Since the foundation of the company, PIQUANT GmbH advises many companies in the areas of Cloud, Collaboration Software, SW development and testing, Service Management, IT Security and IT Architecture and supports them actively during the realization.
All projects were completed with the highest customer satisfaction. Certifications and constant updating of knowledge about standards as well as methods and guidelines are among the main features of the company philosophy.
All PIQUANT employees have to prove corresponding certifications as well as methodological knowledge (ITIL, Scrum, Kanban etc.) before they start working in the consulting business.

IT Infrastructure and Cloud

The highly interesting topic of cloud computing holds both opportunities and risks for many companies and organizations. In general, cloud solutions offer a simplified construction and management of the IT infrastructure. This is initially recognized by many experts. But cloud solutions are associated with several thousand IT artifacts (virtual servers, dockers, disks, storage, etc.), whose architecture must be designed extremely carefully beforehand. Otherwise, a cloud environment will be almost impossible for companies to contain, both in terms of administration and costs. Our competencies include the frequently used SAP and Azure Cloud solutions.
. Here we offer customer-oriented consulting, especially in the area of architecture and migration.

IT Information and Security

Which company or organization does not ask itself the question about the security of its IT infrastructure. And what does a secure IT actually say about information security.
A secure IT or the secure IT technology used says little about the security of information at first.
After all, every information-processing IT artifact or technology (computer, server, database, router, etc.) is nowadays in constant contact with the outside world. Information that is created, processed or modified by us is continuously sent on a journey through the Internet or received on its journey.
And anyone who sends important information through the Internet and/or receives it from the Internet must deal with IT and information security.
PIQUANT advises and develops appropriate solutions in IT and information security. In this area we make use of different guidelines like NIST and standards like ISO 27K.

Open Source and Linux

Giants like Microsoft and Apple dominate the operating system sector, but in our opinion these giants are hardly better than the possibilities available in the open source area.
Let's take only the Linux derivatives (Ubuntu, SuSe, Debian etc.) as examples. All of these systems not only offer almost the same functionality as commercial operating systems, but also a more cost-effective application due to their easy maintenance and the resources required.
Our certified open source specialists and partners are here to show our customers more cost-effective alternatives and advise them.
